Nexus is a cloud-based project management platform designed for teams of all sizes. 
To get started, create an account at nexus.io and choose a plan that fits your team. 
After signing up, you will be prompted to create your first workspace. 
A workspace is a shared environment where your team collaborates on projects. 
You can invite team members by going to Settings, then Team, then clicking Invite Members. 
Enter their email addresses and assign them a role: Admin, Member, or Viewer. 
Admins can manage billing and settings. Members can create and edit projects. Viewers can only read content.
New users receive an email invitation and must accept it before joining the workspace.
Nexus supports Google and Microsoft single sign-on for faster login.
