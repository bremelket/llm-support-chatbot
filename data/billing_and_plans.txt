Nexus offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Enterprise.
The Free plan supports up to 3 users and 5 active projects with 1GB of storage.
The Pro plan costs $12 per user per month and includes unlimited projects, 50GB storage, and priority support.
The Enterprise plan offers custom pricing, SSO, advanced security, and a dedicated account manager.
To upgrade your plan, go to Settings, then Billing, then click Upgrade.
You can pay monthly or annually. Annual billing gives you a 20% discount.
Nexus accepts all major credit cards and invoicing for Enterprise customers.
To cancel your subscription, go to Settings, then Billing, then click Cancel Plan.
After cancellation your workspace remains active until the end of the billing period.
Refunds are available within 7 days of a charge if you have not used more than 10% of your plan's resources.
