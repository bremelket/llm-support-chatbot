To reset your password, click Forgot Password on the login page and enter your email address.
You will receive a reset link valid for 24 hours.
To change your email address, go to Profile Settings and update the email field. A verification email will be sent to the new address.
Two-factor authentication is available under Security Settings. Nexus supports authenticator apps and SMS.
If you lose access to your two-factor device, contact support@nexus.io with your account email and proof of identity.
Admins can remove a team member by going to Settings, then Team, selecting the member, and clicking Remove.
Removed members immediately lose access to the workspace and all its content.
All data in Nexus is encrypted at rest and in transit using AES-256 and TLS 1.3.
Nexus is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.
To export your workspace data, go to Settings, then Data, and click Request Export. You will receive a download link within 24 hours.
